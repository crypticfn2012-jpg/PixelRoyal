Pixel Royale.io
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy describes how Pixel Royale.io, a top-down browser battle royale developed by Ron for PixelForge Studios, handles game-related information.

Information and accounts

The game does not operate its own account server or ask you to create a separate game password. When CrazyGames account integration is available, the game can show the platform's own sign-in prompt and display basic profile details such as your username or profile picture. CrazyGames processes account and technical information under its own policies.

Saved progress and settings

The game saves play statistics, wins, eliminations, coins, selected cosmetics, upgrades, playtime, display preferences, audio options, and control bindings. When running on CrazyGames, it uses the CrazyGames SDK Data Module for guest and signed-in progress where the module is enabled. Outside CrazyGames, progress is stored in browser storage on your device. Clearing browser site data may remove local progress. Cross-device sync depends on the CrazyGames Data Module and its Progress Save setting being enabled for the game.

Platform services and advertisements

Pixel Royale.io can request advertisements through the CrazyGames SDK. CrazyGames controls its account, save, technical, and advertising services and handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Read the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

Children and personal information

The game does not ask players to enter personal details directly. Please follow the age requirements and privacy controls of the platform where you play.

Changes to this page

This policy may be updated as the game or its integrations change. The latest version will be available here.
